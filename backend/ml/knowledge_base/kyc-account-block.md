# KYC and account-block scams

Messages that say your bank account, PAN, or WhatsApp will be blocked unless you update KYC immediately are a common fraud pattern in India.

Complete KYC only inside the official bank or UPI app, or at a branch. Do not tap shortened links (bit.ly, tinyurl) from unknown senders.

CERT-In and Cyberdost advisories repeatedly warn that impersonation of RBI, NPCI, income tax, and electricity boards is widespread. Verify using official websites, not the link in the message.
