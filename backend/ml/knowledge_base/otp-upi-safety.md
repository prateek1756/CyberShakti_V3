# OTP and UPI PIN safety

Genuine banks, UPI apps, and government agencies never ask you to share an OTP, UPI PIN, CVV, or full card number over a phone call, SMS, or WhatsApp.

Entering your UPI PIN authorises a payment. You never need a PIN to receive money. If someone asks you to enter a PIN so they can send you money, it is a scam.

If you already shared an OTP, contact your bank from the number on the back of your card or the official app, and report the incident on the National Cybercrime Helpline 1930.
